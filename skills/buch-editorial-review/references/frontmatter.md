# Front-Matter-Baustein: "Scope, Ethics, and Compliance"

Eine Seite, direkt vor Kapitel 1 oder nach "From the Author". An das jeweilige Buch anpassen — die Aufzählung der beschriebenen Situationen muss zum tatsächlichen Inhalt passen, sonst wirkt sie wie ein Textbaustein.

## Kurzfassung (Denkwerkzeug-Bücher, z. B. Microstandards)

> **Scope, Ethics, and Compliance**
>
> This book presents [Gegenstand] as thinking tools for problem solving in engineering, business, and social-psychological situations.
>
> **Important:** [Gegenstand] are intended for legal, ethical, and compliant use only. They are not an instruction manual for wrongdoing. The author does not recommend deception, disinformation, manipulation, fraud, circumvention of audits or regulations, or any actions that violate laws, contracts, or professional codes.
>
> Some historical and illustrative examples describe how people acted in real situations. These cases are included to explain mechanisms of thinking, to support prevention, and to help readers recognize risky patterns. Readers are responsible for how they apply these tools and should always follow applicable laws, safety requirements, and compliance rules. When in doubt, consult qualified legal, safety, or compliance experts.
>
> *Optional, am Ende:* In professional settings, apply [Gegenstand] transparently and document decisions.

## Langfassung (Fallstudien-/Methodikbücher mit Unfällen und Regulierungsversagen)

> **Scope, Ethics, and Compliance**
>
> This book is a methodological reference. Its subject is [Methode] as applied to [Gegenstand].
>
> Every example in this book is drawn from publicly documented cases: industrial accidents, abandoned engineering projects, regulatory failures, and commercial collapses. They are included because each illustrates a pattern that [Methode] analysis would have identified in advance. They are not included as templates for any reader's conduct.
>
> In particular, this book describes situations in which [an das Buch anpassen: safety budgets were cut below safe levels; testing programmes were curtailed below specification; accident causes were concealed or misattributed; new technologies were suppressed for the benefit of incumbents]. These descriptions are diagnostic, not prescriptive. Wherever the text explains how a failure occurred, the purpose is to help the reader recognise that pattern in their own work and prevent it. Nothing in this book is intended to advise the reader on how to bypass safety regulations, quality standards, regulatory review, environmental compliance, occupational safety law, or any other lawful obligation.
>
> Some examples involve military systems, government programmes, or politically sensitive events. They are included only to illustrate technical, organisational, or decision-making principles — never to endorse any government, ideology, conflict, weapon, or unsafe practice.
>
> Readers working in regulated industries — aerospace, automotive, pharmaceutical, medical device, nuclear, chemical process, food safety, financial services, and others — should treat the analysis in this book as supplementary to, not a substitute for, the applicable regulatory framework, industry standards, and the judgement of qualified specialists licensed in their jurisdiction.
>
> Historical examples are drawn from publicly available sources. Named individuals, organisations, and projects appear because of their established place in the engineering and industrial record. Where the analysis attributes causation to a specific organisational decision, that attribution reflects the published investigation record — regulatory reports, government inquiries, and peer-reviewed engineering analyses. It does not represent a legal conclusion of fault.

## Warum der letzte Absatz wichtig ist

Er trennt "der Untersuchungsbericht sagt X" von "der Autor behauptet X". Bei Büchern, die Unternehmen und Personen namentlich in Unfälle einordnen, ist das der Absatz, der den Unterschied macht.
